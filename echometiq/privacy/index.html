ECHOMETIQ privacy policy

Applies to the ECHOMETIQ app for Android and Windows. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

In short

ECHOMETIQ works on your device. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive your joints, readings, photos or reports.

Who is responsible

nAIbator OÜ, registry code 17359018, Tallinn, Estonia. Contact: info@naibator.com

What the app keeps on your device

  • The joint, probe and material data you enter.
  • Instrument readings you type in and the results calculated from them.
  • Photos you add to a report and the reports you save or export.

This data stays in the app's own storage on your device. It leaves the device only when you export or share a report yourself.

Permissions

The Android app does not ask for internet, camera, location or storage permissions. When you add a photo, the system camera or photo picker hands the app only the picture you chose.

Purchases

ECHOMETIQ is not on sale yet. When it is released, the free trial and the subscription will be sold through Google Play. Google will process the payment under its own privacy policy, and we will not receive your card details. This section will be updated before the first paid version is released.

When you write to us

If you email support, we receive your address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it stored on the device. To have your support emails deleted, write to info@naibator.com.

Children

ECHOMETIQ is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released.
