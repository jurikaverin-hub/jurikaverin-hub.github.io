01 · Joint and probe

Your weld. Your probe. Your material.

Enter the joint, the probe and the material. The beam angle is recalculated for the sound velocity in that metal, and the drawing shows where to scan and how much surface to prepare.

02 · DAC setup

Reference block and probe positions

Plan the reference block before you cut it. Reflectors are spaced for the spread of the beam, and each probe position comes with its path and dimensions.

03 · Analysis

An instrument reading becomes a point on the drawing

Type in the sound path from your flaw detector. ECHOMETIQ places it on your own joint with depth and projection, so you can tell an indication from the geometry of the weld.
