ECHOMETIQ: Guide

PDF

Four modules at the bottom of the screen: Test object, Probe, DAC, Beam. Go left to right: each one uses the data of the one before.

1. Test object: material

1234
  1. 1Select the material
  2. 2Measured the shear velocity? Enter it, m/s
  3. 3Dimensions, preparation and weld are on the next tabs
  4. 4The drawing updates at once

The material sets the sound velocity, and the velocity sets the beam angle. So start here.

2. Test object: dimensions

123
  1. 1Thickness t₁
  2. 2Thickness t₂ unknown? Untick the box: the beam enters it with no reflections calculated
  3. 3Thickness t₂

t₁ is the element the probe moves on. t₂ is the element it is welded to. The tilt γ is set further down on this tab.

3. Test object: preparation and weld

123
  1. 1By the standard or by hand
  2. 2Shape and side of the preparation
  3. 3Weld tab: height and width of the cap

Take the shape from ISO 9692-1 or define it by hand. Below the list you see the dimensions the standard allows.

4. Probe: angle

1234
  1. 1Probe angle in the reference block
  2. 2Calculated angle in the selected material
  3. 3Drag the probe along the weld. A and B: the side
  4. 4Beam, reflections and beam spread

The angle on the probe is valid in the reference block. In another material it differs, and the app recalculates it. The probe index distance is on the Housing tab, the element size on the Beam tab.

5. Probe: position

123
  1. 1Side the probe stands on
  2. 2Distance from the probe front to the edge of the cap
  3. 3How far to draw the beam

Move the probe on the drawing or here. The coloured strips on the drawing are the scanning zone and the zone to clean; their sizes are on the Calculations tab.

6. DAC: reference block

1234
  1. 1Block thickness
  2. 2The length is fitted for you
  3. 3Tap a probe or number: path and dimensions
  4. 4List of all positions

The app places the holes so that the spreading beam does not touch the neighbours. You can also define your own block on the Reflectors tab.

7. DAC: positions

123
  1. 1X: from the index point, Xfront: from the probe front, S: sound path
  2. 2From which side to approach the hole
  3. 3Remove a position from the list

Set the probe at each position, maximise the echo and build the curve on your flaw detector. The app gives the geometry; you measure the amplitudes.

8. Beam: from the sound path

1234
  1. 1SA and model mode
  2. 2Sound path SA from your flaw detector, mm
  3. 3The point on the drawing
  4. 4Position tab: probe side and location

Only the path from the instrument is needed. The app runs the beam through your joint and places the point. It is a model: it shows where the signal may have come from.

9. Beam: from instrument readings

1234
  1. 1Instrument readings mode
  2. 2SA: sound path
  3. 3RA: along the surface from the probe front
  4. 4DA: depth

Does your instrument show SA, RA and DA? Enter all three. The point follows the instrument's numbers, and the Analysis tab compares it with the model and tells you whether the readings agree.

10. Beam → Report

123
  1. 1Photos from the gallery or the camera
  2. 2Save the record on the device
  3. 3Open to print or save as PDF

A record holds the drawing, readings, comment and photos. Enter the operator and organisation once, in Settings.

11. Unknown metal

123
  1. 1X: along the surface to the reflector
  2. 2h: depth of the reflector
  3. 3Estimated velocity and angle

Test object → Additional settings → calculator. Confirm the angle and velocity on the reference block, find the maximum from a known reflector on the part and enter two dimensions. The app estimates the velocity and angle; it does not identify the grade.

If something does not match

  • The point is outside the weld: check side A or B, the probe position and the index distance on the Housing tab.
  • A dash instead of the angle: at this velocity the angle is critical and the beam does not enter the metal. Check the velocity.
  • The readings do not agree: check the instrument calibration and the point it measures RA from.
  • The panel covers the drawing: drag the handle between them.

Important

ECHOMETIQ calculates beam geometry in a homogeneous material. It does not identify defect types and does not replace instrument calibration, an approved procedure or the judgement of a certified inspector.
