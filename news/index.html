Defect or geometry? ECHOMETIQ in 30 seconds

An echo on the screen: a real defect, or just the geometry of the joint? ECHOMETIQ helps you tell, by showing where the beam goes in your joint.

Type in the sound path from your flaw detector. ECHOMETIQ traces the beam through your own joint, probe and material, and puts the reading on the drawing, with its depth and distance from the probe index.

  • Beam angle corrected for the sound velocity in your metal
  • DAC reference block laid out before you machine it
  • Reports with drawing, readings and photos, stored on your device
  • Works offline, in 8 languages

Launching soon on Android (Google Play), Windows to follow. 14-day free trial.
